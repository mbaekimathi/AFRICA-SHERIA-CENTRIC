How this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What we use

This website uses a small number of cookies and browser storage entries to keep the site working and secure:

  • Security cookies — protect forms (such as the enquiry form and sign-in pages) against cross-site request forgery. These are strictly necessary and cannot be switched off.
  • Session cookies — keep you signed in on the client portal and staff workspace. They expire when you sign out or close your browser.
  • Preference storage — remembers display choices such as theme, font, and density in your own browser. These stay on your device and are not sent to us as part of browsing.

What we don't do

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell information about your visit to anyone.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking security or session cookies will stop sign-in and form submissions from working, but the public pages will still be readable.

Questions

For questions about this notice, contact the chambers through the contact page. For how we handle personal information, see our privacy policy.
