Our commitment to a website everyone can use.

Our commitment

{{ firm_name }} aims to make this website usable by everyone, including people using assistive technologies such as screen readers, magnifiers, voice control, and keyboard-only navigation. We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic landmarks, headings in logical order, and a skip-to-content link.
  • Keyboard-operable menus, forms, and interactive controls.
  • Visible focus indicators and text contrast checked against AA.
  • Alternative text on meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from assistive tech.
  • Animations respect your reduced-motion system preference.

Known limitations

Some older content and third-party embeds (such as the map on the contact page) may not yet meet the same standard. We are improving these areas as we update the site.
